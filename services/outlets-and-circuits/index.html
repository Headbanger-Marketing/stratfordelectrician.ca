An extra receptacle does not establish extra capacity

Receptacle locations and circuit design answer different questions. More convenient plug-in points may share the same existing circuit. New appliances or specialty equipment may need a different connection, a dedicated circuit or other assessed work. Give the Licensed Electrical Contractor the equipment information and intended location so the proposal follows the actual use.

Prepare the scope for your Stratford property

List each tool or machine with its rating plate details, photograph the plate where it is easy to read and note which items are likely to run at the same time in the room.

Ask the contractor whether the existing receptacles in the workshop share one circuit, which ones were added later and whether any tool needs its own dedicated circuit rather than a plug-in point.

What should the assessment establish?

A workshop or hobby space in Stratford holds tools and hobby equipment. The circuits already serving that room are identified, along with whatever else they supply in other parts of the home. The contractor sets the equipment list against those circuits and says whether any item needs a dedicated connection. If receptacles were added over time, the scope records how each one was connected. Damaged or unlabelled devices found anywhere in the space are noted, and any new or changed circuit is labelled and written down for the file.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can I add outlets in a workshop without checking what else is on the circuit?

It is better not to. New plug-in points may join a circuit that already serves other rooms, adding places to plug in but no capacity. Ask the contractor to record what each workshop circuit serves first.

Where should rating plate photographs of my hobby equipment be kept?

Keep them in the same folder as the written scope and the circuit labels, with a note of where each machine is used. That way the record of what the circuits were meant to serve stays complete.
